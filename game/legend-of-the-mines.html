Legend of the Mines

This project is a hybrid educational quiz game centered on the industrial mining history of Sungai Lembing. Designed to complement physical museum visits, it requires players to collect information from Muzium Sungai Lembing’s exhibits. The game also features two mining-themed minigames: a puzzle-matching challenge and an arcade claw machine..
